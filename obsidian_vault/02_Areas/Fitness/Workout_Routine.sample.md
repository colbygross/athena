# Weekly Tactical Workout Regimen

## 📅 Rotation Schedule
- **Monday**: Upper Strength
- **Tuesday**: Lower Strength
- **Wednesday**: Zone 2 Aerobic Base
- **Thursday**: Rest & Mobility
- **Friday**: Full-Body Hypertrophy
- **Saturday**: Outdoor Trail Run
- **Sunday**: Active Recovery & Stretching

---

## 🏋️ Routine Details

### Monday
1. Barbell Bench Press (4x6)
2. Barbell Bent-Over Rows (4x8)
3. Overhead Dumbbell Press (3x10)
4. Pull-ups (3xMax)

### Tuesday
1. Barbell Back Squats (4x6)
2. Romanian Deadlifts (4x8)
3. Walking Lunges (3x12/leg)
4. Hanging Knee Tucks (3x15)

### Wednesday
- 45 min Zone 2 Cadence Cycle
- Core Planks (3x60s)

### Thursday
- Hip & Hamstring Foam Rolling
- Deep Squat Mobility (10 min)

### Friday
1. Incline Dumbbell Bench Press (3x10)
2. Barbell Deadlifts (3x5)
3. Neutral Grip Chin-ups (3x8)
4. Lateral Cable Raises (3x12)

### Saturday
- 8km Outdoor Trail Run (Zone 2 - Zone 3)

### Sunday
- 20 min Restorative Hatha Yoga
- 15 min Breathwork
